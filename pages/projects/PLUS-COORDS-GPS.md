# Plus Coords GPS

**Platform:** Android (Native) | **Play Store:** [Plus Coords GPS Data](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.daviddallet.offlinegps) | **Developer page:** [David Dallet on Play Store](https://play.google.com/store/apps/developer?id=David+Dallet)

---

## Overview

A native Android app displaying GPS data, coordinates, and Plus Codes (Open Location Code).

**Key feature:** Works completely offline. Can generate shareable location links without internet access — useful in areas with SMS but no data, or when conserving mobile data.
